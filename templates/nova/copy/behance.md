Title: Nova, Creative Agency Website

Description:
Nova is a website design for creative and marketing agencies that want their work and their people to carry the first impression. Editorial typography and a large, organic image shape on the first screen set a confident tone, followed by a logo strip and key numbers that show experience at a glance.

A selected work section leads with large project images, then a services overview, a team section and an FAQ answer the questions clients ask before a first call. Warm off-white surfaces, deep black blocks and a soft bronze accent give the page a calm, premium character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens. The website has Polish and English versions.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
