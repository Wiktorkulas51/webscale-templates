Title: Grain, Bespoke Furniture Website

Description:
Grain is a website design for bespoke furniture and kitchen makers who want clients to trust the plan before the first cut. In the hero a visitor drags a line across the same kitchen, from the technical drawing on one side to the finished room on the other, with a 3420 mm dimension line running over both. Furniture cards show the price per linear metre. In the kitchen planner a visitor picks an I, L, U or island layout, sets the wall lengths and fronts, and watches a top view plan and an indicative price update. Fronts and worktops hang like sample tags on a ring, and each one straightens when you point at it. The process from measurement to installation sits on dimension lines with the time each stage takes. Forest green and charcoal with Barlow Condensed headings give it a precise, workshop character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
