Title: Grain, Bespoke Furniture Website

Description:
Grain is a website design for bespoke furniture and kitchen makers who want clients to trust the plan before the first cut. In the hero a visitor drags a line across the same kitchen, from the technical drawing on one side to the finished room on the other, with a 3420 mm dimension line running over both.

In the kitchen planner a visitor picks an I, L, U or island layout, sets the wall lengths with sliders and chooses laminate, lacquer or veneer fronts. A top view plan with dimension lines redraws as they go, next to the cabinet run and an indicative price. Furniture cards show the price per linear metre, and kitchens, wardrobes, bathrooms and home offices each get a page with materials, fittings, price and timing. Fronts and worktops hang like sample tags on a ring and straighten when you point at them. The process from measurement to installation sits on dimension lines with the time each stage takes. Client reviews come with a photo of the finished room, and the projects page shows eight interiors with the city and floor area. Six common questions close the page in a FAQ with structured data for search.

Forest green, charcoal and warm off-white, with Barlow Condensed headings and Hanken Grotesk text, give the page a precise, workshop character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
