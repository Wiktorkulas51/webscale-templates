Title: Volta, Electrician Website

Description:
Volta is a website design for electricians and electrical contractors who want customers to see the scope and the paperwork before the first visit. A yellow fault band sits right under the first screen, with the emergency number and safety notes.

The services table shows who each service is for and what it covers, with a separate dark row for emergency call-outs. The process runs like a circuit: a wire passes through three steps and ends at a measurement report, the document customers receive at handover. Recent projects list the property and the scope, and the crew section shows certificates. Graphite, electric yellow and condensed type on a technical grid give the page a precise, trade-ready character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
