Title: Volta, Electrician Website

Description:
Volta is a website design for electricians and electrical contractors who want customers to see the scope and the paperwork before the first visit. A services table shows who each service is for and what it covers, with a separate dark row for emergency call-outs and a yellow fault band right under the first screen. The process runs like a circuit: a wire passes through three steps and ends at a measurement report, the document customers receive at handover. Graphite, electric yellow and condensed type on a technical grid give it a precise, trade-ready character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
