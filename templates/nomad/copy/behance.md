Title: Nomad, Moving Company Website

Description:
Nomad is a website design for moving companies that need visitors to estimate a move quickly and understand the process before they book. The first screen puts a three-step form for route, size and date above the fold, so the next step is clear from the start.

Below it, service packages for flats, houses and offices are laid out side by side, followed by an hour by hour moving day plan that shows what happens on the day. An orange accent, deep navy and condensed type give the page a practical, transport character. The fleet with load capacities and the insurance section answer the questions people ask most often.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
