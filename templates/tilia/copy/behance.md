Title: Tilia, Psychotherapy Practice Website

Description:
Tilia is a website design for psychotherapy practices that see adults, couples and teenagers in person and online, written for someone who is not sure where to start. A three question matcher asks what brings the visitor, how they would like to meet and what time suits them, then suggests a therapist with their next free appointment and price. Anyone who chooses a crisis gets free helpline numbers right away instead of a waiting list.

The rest of the page lowers the threshold step by step. A short list shows the first sentences people often say, so the visitor does not need a name for what is happening. Therapist cards show approach, languages and the next slot, the first meeting is described as a conversation rather than an exam, and a price table with the cancellation rule and an FAQ answer the practical questions. Soft linen tones, deep navy and Spectral serif headings give the page a quiet, reassuring character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
