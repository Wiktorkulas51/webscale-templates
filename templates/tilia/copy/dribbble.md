Title: Tilia, Psychotherapy Practice Website

Description:
Tilia is a website design for psychotherapy practices that see adults, couples and teenagers in person and online, written for someone who is not sure where to start. A three question matcher asks what brings the visitor, how they would like to meet and what time suits them, then suggests a therapist with their next free appointment and price. Anyone who chooses a crisis gets free helpline numbers right away instead of a waiting list. A short list of the first sentences people often say, therapist cards with approach, languages and the next slot, a calm description of the first meeting, a price table with the cancellation rule and an FAQ follow. Soft linen tones, deep navy and Spectral serif headings give it a quiet, reassuring character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
