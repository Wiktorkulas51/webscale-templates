Title: Lex, Law Office Website

Description:
Lex is a website design for law offices that serve people and businesses and want a worried visitor to find a clear next step. Right below the hero, a deadline calculator asks what letter arrived from the court and when, then shows the last day to respond, the days left, what is worth doing today and whether the deadline moves past a weekend. Practice areas are split into matters for people and for companies, a fee table lays out four ways of charging, and a checklist shows which documents to bring to the first meeting for family, inheritance, debt or company matters. Attorney profiles list focus and languages, and the contact section is set like a letterhead. Prussian blue on warm paper with Schibsted Grotesk headings give it a calm, precise character, with no star ratings. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
