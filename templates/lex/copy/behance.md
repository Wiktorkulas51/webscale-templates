Title: Lex, Law Office Website

Description:
Lex is a website design for law offices that serve people and businesses and want a worried visitor to find a clear next step. Right below the hero, a deadline calculator asks what letter arrived from the court and when, then shows the last day to respond, the days left and what is worth doing today. If the deadline falls on a day off, it moves to the next working day and says so.

Practice areas are split into matters for people and for companies, and a fee table lays out four ways of charging, from a single consultation to ongoing business support. A checklist shows which documents to bring to the first meeting for family, inheritance, debt or company matters. Attorney profiles list focus and languages, the FAQ answers the questions asked before a first meeting, and the contact section is set like a letterhead. Prussian blue on warm paper with Schibsted Grotesk headings give the page a calm, precise character, with no star ratings.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
