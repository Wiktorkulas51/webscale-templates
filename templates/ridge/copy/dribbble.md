Title: Ridge, Roofing Company Website

Description:
Ridge is a website design for roofing and sheet metal companies that want homeowners to understand the job before they ask for a quote. A roof cross-section walks through each layer from the rafters to the roofing, with what the crew does there and which material goes in. In the roof cost estimator a visitor sets the roof area with a slider, picks the scope and the material, and sees a price range and the number of working days. Services show prices per square metre, a map pins recent jobs across the region with scope, duration and material, and the process runs up a slope from the inspection to the handover. Slate ink, copper flashing and warm paper with condensed Bebas Neue headings give it a sturdy, craftsman character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
