Title: Ridge, Roofing Company Website

Description:
Ridge is a website design for roofing and sheet metal companies that want homeowners to understand the job before they ask for a quote. Below the hero, a roof cross-section walks through each layer from the rafters to the roofing, with what the crew does there and which material goes in.

The roof cost estimator answers the first question every homeowner has. A slider sets the roof area, the visitor picks the scope and the material, and the page shows a price range and the number of working days before anyone picks up the phone. Services show prices per square metre, a map pins recent jobs across the region with scope, duration and material, and the process runs up a slope from the inspection to the handover, with the time each stage takes. Slate ink, copper flashing and warm paper with condensed Bebas Neue headings give the page a sturdy, craftsman character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
