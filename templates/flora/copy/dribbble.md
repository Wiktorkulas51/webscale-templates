Title: Flora, Florist Website

Description:
Flora is a website design for florists and flower studios that sell same-day bouquets and wedding flowers. The logo sits in the middle of the menu, and the hero lays bouquet prints on a sage field like photos on a studio table, with the delivery cut-off right under them. Occasions have clear prices, the bouquet of the day sits on a raspberry band, and a composer suggests three bouquets by occasion, colour and budget, then fills in the order form. A simple city map shows delivery zones with prices and time windows. Raspberry rose, sage and cream with the Spectral serif give it a warm, handmade character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
