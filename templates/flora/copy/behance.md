Title: Flora, Florist Website

Description:
Flora is a website design for florists and flower studios that sell same-day bouquets and wedding flowers. The logo sits in the middle of the menu, and the hero lays bouquet prints on a sage field like photos on a studio table, with the delivery cut-off right under them.

Occasion tiles show a price for each moment: birthdays, love and apologies, sympathy and business. The bouquet of the day sits on a raspberry band with its flowers and the time it is available until. A composer suggests three bouquets by occasion, colour and budget, and sends the choice to the order form. A simple city map shows delivery zones with prices and time windows, and the wedding section pairs a decorated hall with packages and a quote form. An office subscription and client reviews close the page.

Raspberry rose, sage and cream, with Spectral headings and Hanken Grotesk text, give the page a warm, handmade character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
