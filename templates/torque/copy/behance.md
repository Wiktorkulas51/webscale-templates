Title: Torque, Car Repair Workshop Website

Description:
Torque is a website design for car repair workshops whose customers are afraid of an inflated bill. The dark hero sets a vertical outlined TORQUE wordmark beside a mechanic checking a car on the lift, with the line "You know the price before we start", a rating and two short promises on the photo.

A price list shows eight common jobs, from an oil change to a timing belt, with the usual time and the starting price. The repair order builder is the heart of the page: tick the jobs the car needs and a printed order card with a perforated edge, an order number and a date lists each line and adds up an estimated total, with a note that the final price is confirmed in writing after diagnosis. A workshop gallery shows four bays, a red band holds four promises, and their icons turn like a bolt being tightened when you point at them. Driver reviews carry the car model and date, and questions before the visit close the page with FAQ structured data. Three service pages each have a scope, time and price table, and a recent repairs page lists problem, diagnosis, cost and time for each job.

Graphite, light grey and one workshop red, with Oswald headings and Instrument Sans text, give the page a direct, honest character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
