Title: Torque, Car Repair Workshop Website

Description:
Torque is a website design for car repair workshops whose customers are afraid of an inflated bill. The dark hero sets a vertical outlined TORQUE wordmark beside a mechanic checking a car on the lift, with the line "You know the price before we start". A price list shows eight common jobs with the usual time and the starting price. The repair order builder is the heart of the page: tick the jobs the car needs and a printed order card with a perforated edge lists each line and adds up an estimated total, ready to reserve a slot. A red band holds four promises, and the nut in the logo turns a little when you point at it, like a bolt being tightened. Graphite, light grey and one workshop red with Oswald headings give it a direct, honest character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
