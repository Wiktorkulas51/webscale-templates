Title: Forma, Construction and Renovation Website

Description:
Forma is a website design for construction and renovation companies that want visitors to see finished spaces first. The first screen pairs a large project photo with a clear headline and a direct call to discuss the work, followed by service cards for home construction, renovations and interior finishing.

A four-step process section explains the path from the first conversation to handover, and an FAQ covers estimates, materials and scheduling. Light surfaces, graphite typography and an amber accent give the page a calm, architectural character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens. The website has Polish and English versions.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.

Creative Fields: Web Design, UI/UX
