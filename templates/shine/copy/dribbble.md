Title: Shine, Cleaning Services Website

Description:
Shine is a website design for cleaning companies, from home and office cleaning to post-renovation work. The hero offers a quick estimate, and a before and after comparison shows the results before visitors read the details. Indicative prices per square metre come with a clear description of the scope, and a process section explains the path from the first enquiry to the cleaning date. Clear typography, a turquoise accent and deep navy give it a fresh, local character, and the layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
