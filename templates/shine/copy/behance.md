Title: Shine, Cleaning Services Website

Description:
Shine is a website design for cleaning companies, from home and office cleaning to post-renovation work. The hero offers a quick way to start an enquiry, and a before and after comparison shows the results before visitors read the details.

Each cleaning type comes with a clear description of its scope, so visitors know what is included before they get in touch. A process section explains the path from the first enquiry to the cleaning date. Clear typography, a turquoise accent and deep navy give the page a fresh, local character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
