Title: Blade, Barbershop Website

Description:
Blade is a website design for barbershops that want a visitor to book a chair in a minute instead of calling. The hero shows the next free slot and the earliest time of each barber today, so the first screen already answers "when can I come in?".

Booking takes three steps. The visitor picks a barber or any barber, a service from the price list and a day and an hour, while a summary card keeps the barber, the service, the price and the duration in view. Every service lists its time and price, service pages walk through the visit and suggest a barber, and the gallery shows cuts done in the shop rather than catalogue photos. Products from the shelf, reviews and the location with opening hours close the page. Dark green and cream with bold Bricolage Grotesque headings give it a sharp, confident character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens, with a booking bar on the phone.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
