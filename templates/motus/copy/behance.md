Title: Motus, Physiotherapy Practice Website

Description:
Motus is a website design for physiotherapy practices that want new patients to know what happens before they book. The first screen pairs a calm headline with a first visit card on the photo, showing the price, three stages and a clear next step.

Below, the first visit is told stage by stage beside a sticky photo that changes with each step. The team, a price list with visit lengths and short patient stories follow. A movement line runs down the margin from the headline to the last section, and deep teal with a coral accent and the Literata serif give the page a calm, caring character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens, with an app-style bottom bar on phones.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
