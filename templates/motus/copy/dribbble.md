Title: Motus, Physiotherapy Practice Website

Description:
Motus is a website design for physiotherapy practices that want new patients to know what happens before they book. The first screen pairs a calm headline with a first visit card on the photo: price, three stages and a clear next step. Below, the first visit is told stage by stage beside a sticky photo, followed by the team, a price list with visit lengths and short patient stories. A movement line running down the margin, deep teal with a coral accent and the Literata serif give it a calm, caring character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens, with an app-style bottom bar on phones.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
