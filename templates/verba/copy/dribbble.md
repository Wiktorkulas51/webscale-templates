Title: Verba, Language School Website

Description:
Verba is a website design for language schools that teach small groups and want a new student to find the right group in a few minutes. A six question level test ends with the student's level and the groups that match it. The schedule of new groups filters by lesson day and shows places left, and course bands for children, teens, adults and companies give the price per semester and per lesson. Teachers greet visitors in the language they teach, exam results count every student rather than only the best, and student stories show the level they started and finished at. Sunny yellow, warm paper and Red Hat Display with Newsreader italics for foreign words give it a lively, welcoming character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
