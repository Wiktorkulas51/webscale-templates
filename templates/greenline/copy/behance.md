Title: Greenline, Gardening and Garden Care Website

Description:
Greenline is a website design for garden design, planting and seasonal garden care businesses that want their finished work to come first. The layout pairs a clear services overview with a projects gallery, so visitors see the results before they read the details.

A process section walks through the steps from first contact to completed work, and a contact form closes the page. Calm greenery, light surfaces and a clear green accent give the page a fresh, natural character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
