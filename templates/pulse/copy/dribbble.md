Title: Pulse, Personal Trainer Website

Description:
Pulse is a website design for personal trainers and dietitians who want new clients to book a first session without pressure. The dark hero sets the word "Move" as a huge outline running across a photo of a coached kettlebell squat. Below it a ticker of services with prices moves slowly and stops when you point at it. The weekly schedule is the heart of the page: every slot shows its length as an orange bar, booked slots are struck through, and choosing a free time carries the day, hour and session type into the booking form. Three packages are laid out week by week, so the difference between a plan and regular sessions is visible at a glance. Graphite with one orange accent and Archivo Black headings give it a bold, focused character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
