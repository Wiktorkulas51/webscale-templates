Title: Pulse, Personal Trainer Website

Description:
Pulse is a website design for personal trainers and dietitians who want new clients to book a first session without pressure. The dark hero sets the word "Move" as a huge outline running across a photo of a coached kettlebell squat, with the rest of the headline and one clear call to pick a time.

The weekly schedule is the heart of the page. Monday to Saturday sits on a light grid, every slot shows its length as an orange bar that redraws when you point at it, and booked slots are struck through. Choosing a free time carries the day, hour and session type into the first session form, where visitors pick a goal and their experience with large buttons. On a phone the grid turns into a day switcher with one list of times.

Three packages are laid out week by week, filled marks for sessions in the studio and outlined marks for work with the plan, and the plan plays through week by week on hover. Client stories sit in a stack of cards with arrows, each describing the starting point, what was agreed and what changed in the week, without before and after photos. The services page compares eight formats in one table, followed by payment terms and a short guide to choosing. Three service pages cover personal training with a minute-by-minute first session, online training and nutrition.

Graphite with one orange accent, Archivo Black headings and Figtree text give the page a bold, focused character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
