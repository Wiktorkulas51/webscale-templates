Title: Lane, Driving School Website

Description:
Lane is a website design for driving schools that need students and parents to pick a course and a start date without calling first. A licence category switch in the header filters the start dates board, the price tabs and the sign-up form at once, so the page always talks about the course the visitor chose. A route to the licence in six stops shows time and cost from sign-up to the exam, followed by instructors and first-attempt pass rates. Graphite asphalt, a green go light and the bold Unbounded typeface give it a confident, road-ready character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
