Title: Lane, Driving School Website

Description:
Lane is a website design for driving schools that need students and parents to pick a course and a start date without calling first. A licence category switch in the header filters the start dates board, the price tabs and the sign-up form at once.

Below the hero, a route to the licence in six stops shows what happens from sign-up to the exam, with the time and cost of each step. Category tabs lay out the price packages, and the instructors and first-attempt pass rates answer the questions parents ask most often. Graphite asphalt, a green go light and the bold Unbounded typeface give the page a confident, road-ready character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
