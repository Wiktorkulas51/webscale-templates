Title: Fauna, Veterinary Clinic Website

Description:
Fauna is a website design for veterinary clinics that want a warm, trustworthy first impression. The first screen leads with a clear call to book a visit and an emergency line for pets that feel unwell. A pet switch shows services and vaccination prices for dogs, cats, rabbits and exotic pets, so owners find the right information without searching through long lists.

The colour and illustration direction comes from warm apricot, deep navy and a friendly dog and cat mascot. Rounded shapes and generous spacing keep the page calm, while a price table, a four-step first visit guide and a styled map answer the questions owners ask most.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens. The website has Polish and English versions.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
