Title: Ledger, Accounting Office Website

Description:
Ledger is a website design for accounting offices that keep the books for sole traders and companies and want a visitor to see the scope and the fee before the first call. An audience switch shows what the office does for sole traders, limited companies, e-commerce and medical practices.

The fee calculator turns pricing into a quick answer. A visitor sets the number of documents, employees and civil contracts, sees the monthly net fee, and the matching plan lights up in the price table below. A tax calendar follows the chosen form of business, marks what is already filed and counts the days to the next deadline. For clients who are changing offices, a four step switch explains the handover, and the team section introduces the people who sign the returns. Burgundy accents on bone paper with Newsreader headings give the page a calm, trustworthy character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
