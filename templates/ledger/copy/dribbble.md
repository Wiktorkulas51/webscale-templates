Title: Ledger, Accounting Office Website

Description:
Ledger is a website design for accounting offices that keep the books for sole traders and companies and want a visitor to see the scope and the fee before the first call. An audience switch shows what the office does for sole traders, limited companies, e-commerce and medical practices. In the fee calculator a visitor sets the number of documents, employees and civil contracts, and the matching plan lights up in the price table below. A tax calendar follows the chosen form of business, marks what is filed and counts the days to the next deadline, and a four step switch explains how the books move over from another office. Burgundy accents on bone paper with Newsreader headings give it a calm, trustworthy character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
