Title: Sprout, Preschool and Nursery Website

Description:
Sprout is a website design for private preschools and nurseries that want parents to know if there is a place for their child before they call. In the group finder a parent picks the child's month and year of birth and sees the group, the free places and the monthly cost after subsidies.

The rest of the page answers what parents ask on a first visit. Four age groups show their teachers, group size and places left. A daily rhythm strip runs from breakfast to afternoon play, and this week's menu highlights today. The teachers, parent reviews, a parent FAQ and an enrolment calendar with dates and steps close the page. Fresh green, sky blue and warm cream with wide Unbounded headings give it a friendly, cheerful character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
