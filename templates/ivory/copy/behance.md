Title: Ivory, Dental Clinic Website

Description:
Ivory is a website design for dental clinics that need to explain treatments and prices without overwhelming patients. The main idea is a smile map that lets visitors pick an area and see its services with prices. Tabbed price lists and a before and after slider support that idea, so patients can compare options before they book.

A cobalt and sand palette, the Red Hat Display typeface and a bento layout keep the page calm and modern. A dental team section and a children's duty block show who the clinic is and how it works for families.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens. The website has Polish and English versions.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
