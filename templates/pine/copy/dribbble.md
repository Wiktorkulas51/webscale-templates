Title: Pine, Mountain Guesthouse Website

Description:
Pine is a website design for small mountain guesthouses that want guests to picture the stay before they book. Next to the hero photo, a column of trail signposts gives the walking time to the peak, the temple and the waterfall, each marked in its trail colour, above a short arrival, departure and guests bar. Room cards show guests, size and the price per night. In the trails section a visitor switches between summer and winter, and every route lists its time, distance and level. A hosts section, guest reviews and a short availability form close the page. Forest green and cream with Oswald headings give it a calm, outdoor character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
