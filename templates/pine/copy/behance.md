Title: Pine, Mountain Guesthouse Website

Description:
Pine is a website design for small mountain guesthouses that want guests to picture the stay before they book. Next to the hero photo, a column of trail signposts gives the walking time to the peak, the temple and the waterfall, each marked in its trail colour, above a short arrival, departure and guests bar.

Room cards show guests, size and the price per night, and each room has its own page with amenities and an enquiry button. In the trails section a visitor switches between summer and winter photos, while the route list keeps the time, distance and level of every trail. The hosts section introduces the people who run the house, followed by guest reviews, an area page with nearby sights and a short availability form with dates and room choice.

Forest green, cream and the red, blue and yellow of mountain trail marks, with Oswald headings and Source Sans 3 text, give the page a calm, outdoor character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
