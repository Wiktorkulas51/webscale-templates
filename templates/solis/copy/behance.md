Title: Solis, Solar Installer Website

Description:
Solis is a website design for solar energy installers who want homeowners to trust the numbers before the first visit. The hero puts the key figures of a typical home system next to a house under a sun arc, and the page background moves from morning to evening as you scroll.

In "The sun over your roof" a visitor picks a month and a time of day. The sun moves along its arc, and the panel shows what the roof makes, what the house uses and what is left for a battery or the grid. Solar panels, heat pumps and home batteries each get a page with a cost, subsidy and payback table. The subsidy guide is a timeline that lights up step by step, with who does what, how long it takes and the amount. Recent projects show the system size and the yearly saving, and a free audit tab stays at hand on every page.

Deep petrol and warm peach, with Sora headings and Schibsted Grotesk text, give the page a calm, honest character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
