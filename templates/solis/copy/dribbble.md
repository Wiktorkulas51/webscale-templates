Title: Solis, Solar Installer Website

Description:
Solis is a website design for solar energy installers who want homeowners to trust the numbers before the first visit. The hero puts the key figures of a typical home system next to a house under a sun arc, and the page background moves from morning to evening as you scroll. In "The sun over your roof" a visitor picks a month and a time of day and sees what the panels make, what the house uses and what is left for a battery or the grid. Subsidies are a step-by-step timeline that lights up as you read it. Deep petrol and warm peach with Sora headings give it a calm, honest character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
