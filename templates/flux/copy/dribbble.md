Title: Flux, Plumbing Company Website

Description:
Flux is a website design for plumbing and heating companies that take emergency calls as well as planned work. The hero splits visitors in two: "I have a leak now" puts the emergency number one tap away, and "I'm planning work" leads to the services. In the emergency section a visitor picks their district and sees the estimated arrival time. Planned work shows how long each of six jobs takes and what it costs, an indicative price table explains where prices start, and a four stage process uses photos from jobs to show how the crew works in a home. Reviews and an FAQ about call-out fees, invoices and warranty close the page. A bold Sora typeface and a clear emergency call to action give it a fast, dependable character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
