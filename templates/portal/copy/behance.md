Title: Portal, Windows and Doors Website

Description:
Portal is a website design for window and door companies that sell from a showroom and want visitors to book a measurement. The hero sets a daylit living room inside a window frame, so the first thing you see is the light a good window lets in.

Two paths split people building a new home from those replacing old windows: design and opening sizes on one side, existing dimensions and protecting the rooms on the other. The catalogue groups PVC and aluminium windows, exterior and interior doors, garage doors, shutters and insect screens, layered installation and service. In the navy showroom section, colour swatches fan out like a sample book when you point at them, next to questions worth asking the adviser and the showroom hours. The process runs from a 15 minute call to delivery and fitting, with the timing on each step, and a small window glyph opens like a sash on hover. Six questions on Uw values, dust, delivery, warranty and tax relief close the page in a FAQ with structured data for search. Product pages explain Uw requirements, glazing, burglar resistance and warm, three-layer installation in plain language.

Navy, oak and daylight grey, with Chivo headings and Source Sans 3 text, give the page a calm, trustworthy character.

The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
