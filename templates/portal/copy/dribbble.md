Title: Portal, Windows and Doors Website

Description:
Portal is a website design for window and door companies that sell from a showroom and want visitors to book a measurement. The hero sets a daylit living room inside a window frame, so the first thing you see is the light a good window lets in. Two paths split people building a new home from those replacing old windows, each with what to prepare before the measurement. The catalogue groups windows, doors and garage doors, and installation. In the navy showroom section, colour swatches fan out like a sample book when you point at them, next to questions worth asking the adviser. The process shows three steps with how long each takes, and a small window glyph opens like a sash on hover. Navy, oak and daylight grey with Chivo headings give it a calm, trustworthy character. The layout is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
Photos were created with the help of AI image tools and edited for the layout.
